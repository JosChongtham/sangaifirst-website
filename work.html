26 Sep

Sangai Day at school

L. Dhananjoy Memorial English School, Tronglaobi

What happened

  • A one-day sensitisation programme for Sangai Day at L. Dhananjoy Memorial English School (LDMES), Tronglaobi.
  • Kumam Jugeshor set out the Sangai’s historical, ecological and cultural importance, and the threats it faces: human pressure, habitat loss linked to the Ithai Barrage, and a population so small it risks inbreeding.

11 Jul

Native tree drive

Thanga Chingjao Hillock, Loktak

What happened

  • A native tree drive at the summit of Thanga Chingjao Hillock, overlooking Loktak Lake, with the Loktak Folklore Museum.
  • It began with the planting of Uningthou (Phoebe hainesiana), Manipur’s State Tree, by Tongbram Ahongjao, founder of the museum, and Kumam Jugeshor.
  • Museum members and our trustees then planted indigenous saplings across the hilltop, to help the hillock’s native forest grow back.

Photos

Who was there

  • Tongbram Ahongjao, Founder, Loktak Folklore Museum
  • Kumam Jugeshor, Vice-President
  • Trustees of the Foundation

24 Nov 2024

Consultation on Sangai Conservation

Dr Shamungou’s home, Nambol Makha Leikai

What happened

  • Earlier observances such as World Wetlands Day had stayed at the level of words, not sustained action.
  • Proposal to mark “Sangai Day” every 10 December, recalling the 1954 resolution to create a sanctuary for the Sangai.
  • Agreement that school students must be at the heart of any long-term conservation effort.
  • Decision to start a trust, later registered as Sangai First Foundation, to run year-long programmes.

Who was there

  • Dr Kh. Shamungou
  • Wildlife enthusiasts, teachers and conservation workers
