How well do you know the Sangai?

Discover more ›

  • QUESTION 1 OF 5

    How many Sangai did the 2026 census find in the wild?

    172

    Down from 260 in 2016. Every one lives in Keibul Lamjao.

  • QUESTION 2 OF 5

    What does the name “Sangai” mean?

    The one that waits

    From “sa” (animal) and “ngai” (awaiting): a running stag stops to look back.

  • QUESTION 3 OF 5

    How thick is the floating phumdi the Sangai walks on?

    Up to 1.2 m

    Only about a fifth of it sits above the water, enough to carry a deer.

  • QUESTION 4 OF 5

    When does the Sangai like to graze?

    Dawn and dusk

    Roughly 4:30 to 8 am and 3 to 6 pm, resting under tall reeds in between.

  • QUESTION 5 OF 5

    Which sport was born in Manipur as Sagol Kangjei?

    Polo

    Manipur is known as the birthplace of modern polo.
